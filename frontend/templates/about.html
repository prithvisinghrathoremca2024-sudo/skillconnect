About SkillConnect

SkillConnect is a social-impact marketplace bridging local low-income workers with nearby customers who need reliable, affordable services.

The Problem

Low-income skilled workers—such as carpenters, plumbers, tailors, and electricians—often lack digital visibility and rely on irregular local word-of-mouth jobs.

Our Solution

We give local service providers a platform to create profiles, showcase ratings, receive local work orders, and earn a sustainable income.

Transparent Revenue Model

SkillConnect charges a 10% platform commission on successfully completed services to manage operations and growth, leaving 90% directly to the worker.
